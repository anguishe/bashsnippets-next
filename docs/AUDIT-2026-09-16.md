## Executive Summary
The site is technically sound. The build, lint and types are clean. All 79 URLs return 200 with `index, follow`. The toolkit CTA is on 77 pages and the email form on 74, and there are no ads or affiliate links. It still earns nothing, for three reasons:

- **Nothing has been distributed.** The dev.to queue due to start Fri 9/11 has not started; the last dev.to post is from 2026-07-30.
- **Google still sends zero clicks.**
- **The one sales page makes three claims the product doesn't back up.**

The site is ready to sell. What's missing is readers and a sales page that tells the truth.

**Top 5 critical issues:**
1. **The distribution plan never ran.** 0 of the 4 dev.to posts due by today are up (POST-QUEUE §A, all `[ ]`; dev.to API newest = 2026-07-30). The 10/06 kill-signal read therefore can't mean anything.
2. **`/starter-kit` says "MIT license"** in 4 places plus the FAQ schema and `llms.txt`. The zip actually ships a single-buyer license that forbids redistribution.
3. **`/starter-kit` says "passes ShellCheck with no suppressions."** The zip has 13 `# shellcheck disable` lines.
4. **Sitewide titles and descriptions break Bing's limits** (34/79 titles over 65 characters, 68/79 descriptions over 160). Bing is the only channel with measured traffic.
5. **The only buy button is 858 words down `/starter-kit`**, and the page new subscribers land on never links to the toolkit in its prose.

**Top 5 quick wins (< 30 min each):**
1. Make the license wording match the zip, either on the page or in the license (Travis picks which), and drop "no suppressions".
2. Add a buy button under the price line on `/starter-kit`, pointed at `…/l/toolkit?wanted=true`.
3. On `/snippets/bashlib-starter`: hide the email form and link the FAQ answer to `/starter-kit`.
4. Link `ports-audit` from `/guides/open-ports-linux` and `service-watchdog` from `/guides/auto-restart-linux-service`.
5. Travis: upload the patched toolkit zip to Gumroad (HANDOFF item 10, open since 9/11) and turn on Gumroad Discover.

## 6. Sales-page truthfulness (`/starter-kit`, the only page that sells)
**FAIL (High): license claim.**
- "MIT license" appears at `src/app/starter-kit/page.tsx:44` (FAQ answer → FAQPage schema), `:139`, `:262`, `:333`, `:347` and in `public/llms.txt:124`.
- `Production-Bash-Toolkit/LICENSE.txt` in the zip says: *"Resell, redistribute, sublicense, or give away the kit itself … ONE BUYER, ONE LICENSE."* That is not MIT.
- A buyer who reads the page and then the file has been told two different things. AI assistants quote the FAQ schema verbatim.

**FAIL (Medium): "Every file passes ShellCheck with no suppressions"** (`page.tsx:59`, `:296-297`). The zip has 13 `# shellcheck disable` lines: bashlib.sh 8, healthcheck.sh 2, backup.sh 2, template.sh 1. "ShellCheck-clean" is true (`shellcheck -x -S style` exits 0); "no suppressions" is not.
**FAIL (Medium): "Tested on…" claims with no run behind them, against the real-runs-only rule.**
- `page.tsx:59` and `:332`: Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12, macOS.
- `src/app/about/page.tsx:196-197`: Ubuntu, Debian 12, Fedora 39, macOS Ventura.
- `src/app/snippets/page.tsx:13/20/29` (meta description).
- `public/llms.txt:7`.

The site's own `/snippets/bashlib-starter` FAQ says "only been tested on Kali Linux". *Inferred* that the runs never happened: no evidence exists in the repo, memory or docs.
**CONCERN: "31-function shared library."** `bashlib.sh` in the current zip has **30** function definitions (grep, both zips). The claim appears in `ToolkitCTA.tsx:32` (77 pages), `starter-kit/page.tsx:174/236/330`, `bashlib-starter.mdx:17/340` and `llms.txt:124`. Recount by hand before changing it; it may count an alias.
